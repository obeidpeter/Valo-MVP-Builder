# Business Plan v1.1 impact update

This is a product/technology impact note, not a revised financial forecast. None of the Business Plan's revenue, conversion, margin, probability, market-size, channel or partner assumptions has been validated by this implementation work.

## Baseline discrepancy

TRD v1.0 states alignment to Business Plan v1.2 and Product Roadmap v1.1, but only Business Plan v1.1 and Roadmap v1.0 were supplied/found. Those newer versions may contain decisions unknown to this work. Treat this update as an implementation addendum to BP v1.1 pending owner reconciliation, not as a silent v1.2 replacement.

## Technology and operating-cost implications

The BP's founder-built, low-capital premise should be re-estimated after Foundation. Production-grade tenancy/RLS, malware scanning, durable jobs, object storage, external audit anchoring, identity/MFA, observability, backup/restore, provider contracts, accessibility/security testing and privacy/legal work add cost and specialised operational burden. No amount is inserted here without quotations and approval.

Required model additions:

- Environment-by-environment infrastructure and provider budget, including non-production.
- Storage/egress/OCR/model/render/notification/payment/monitoring/backup/security-scan unit costs.
- Staff/on-call, DPO/DPCO/legal, penetration/accessibility testing, insurance and incident reserve.
- Cost of manual human QA and partner co-sign at each product tier.
- Data residency/restricted-mode premium based on actual architecture, not marketing assumption.
- Sensitivity to tender page count, scanned-page ratio, retry rate, deadline peaks and provider FX pricing.

## Pricing and entitlements

Existing prices remain planning assumptions only. Product logic must use a versioned price book and entitlement policy. Platform subscription and service retainer are separate products; order lines state included human service, usage, fair-use, SLA and overflow treatment. Provider or tax changes create new effective price/tax versions; historical invoices remain tied to prior versions.

## Commercial activation gates

Technical release does not satisfy these real-world gates. Owners must record actual evidence for:

- Five real Autopsies delivered within the agreed SLA.
- Paid engagements, retainers, conversion and on-time delivery.
- Valo-caused disqualification rate and quality incidents.
- Client/partner consent and support readiness.
- Partner/channel/new-logo and revenue-mix criteria.
- Replacement-cost crossover and margin/unit cost.

This dossier neither confirms nor rejects the numerical BP targets; it prevents them from being represented as test results.

## Claims/source changes

- Re-verify 2026 budget, MDA count, BPP thresholds, NCDMB/NipeX claims, local-content trajectory, category adoption/win-rate studies and compliance certificate generalisations before external use.
- Procurement/tax/certificate rules must cite authoritative current sources/tender instructions through signed rule packs. The BP appendix is a working taxonomy, not executable law.
- Claims such as “any Nigerian tender,” “no controllable defect survives,” 95% recall, 100% on-time or specific availability must be scoped to measured cohorts/service conditions and accompanied by limitations.
- Repository privacy and IP handling require immediate owner confirmation because the local archive does not prove GitHub visibility/permissions.

## Governance/KPI changes

Add leading indicators: citation correctness; fatal/likely-fatal recall; unsupported-claim rejection; BOQ fixture detection; tenant-denial tests; visual QA completion; accessibility defects; job/provider failure/recovery; backup restore/rollback; audit-anchor freshness; privacy requests/incidents; feature-flag exposure; partner segregation; benchmark suppression/withdrawal. Report dataset/sample limitations beside every AI/quality percentage.

## Recommended BP revision disposition

- **Retain:** integrity red lines, service-first sequencing, Autopsy wedge, evidence-grounded differentiation, licensed discovery, human QA.
- **Redesign:** technology effort/cost, portal/channel scope, privacy/anonymisation, warranties/claims, operating model and release calendar.
- **Add:** multi-tenant governance, provider/adaptor/DR/security operations, commercial-vs-technical flags, current Nigeria rule packs and repository security.
- **Remove:** fixed legal/retention/anonymity constants presented without current authority; any broad availability/quality claim not measured.
- **Defer:** GCC implementation and other post-v2.5 expansion.
