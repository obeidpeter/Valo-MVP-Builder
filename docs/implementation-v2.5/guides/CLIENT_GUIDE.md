# Client guide

## Start and invite

Complete organisation identity, MFA, NDA/privacy and member roles. Invite only people who need the workspace and review access regularly. Your organisation owns its documents; Valo processes them under the agreed purposes and never uses tenant data to train shared models.

## Create an engagement

Identify the tender and exact lot, deadline and service. Valo checks same-tender conflicts and entitlement before work starts. A block explains the reason and safe next action; it is not a technical error to bypass.

## Upload safely

Use the portal's resumable upload. Wait for server receipt; “uploaded” is followed by quarantine/security/format processing. Do not send files to personal accounts. Password-protected/corrupt/unsupported/duplicate/addendum states provide specific recovery. New evidence remains unverified until an authorised reviewer approves it.

## Complete actions

The readiness view shows requirements, owner, due date, evidence and block reason. Supply authentic current evidence and authorised instructions. Do not ask Valo to invent experience, credentials, rates, relationships or evaluator intelligence. Comments and revisions remain attributable.

## Review and approve

Open sources/evidence and changes, not just summaries. A fatal/likely-fatal issue, unsupported claim, unresolved placeholder, BOQ exception or missing approval prevents readiness. Client approval does not replace required Valo quality sign-off.

## Download and privacy

Packages are immutable versions with signed expiring links; verify filename/version/manifest and store under your policy. Request access/correction/deletion/support through the portal. Legal/contractual holds or minimum accountability records may limit deletion and will be explained in the decision/certificate.
