# Reviewer and quality-adviser guide

## Requirement review

Confirm the exact source version, view the page/snippet/coordinate, and decide confirm/edit/reject/merge/split. Preserve citations when merging. AI confidence is triage, not truth. Check mandatory/severity/category/evidence/due date; explain material edits. If an addendum or stale version appears, stop and reopen affected work.

## Evidence and capability

Verify issuer/source, authenticity method, relevant dates, tender scope, restrictions and fact-level support. Approval identity and time are recorded. Expired/rejected/withdrawn/unverified evidence cannot ground a released claim. Do not approve your own material claim as sole evidence and final signer.

## Defects and reclassification

Tie every defect to requirement/source/evidence, taxonomy and remediation. Fatal/likely-fatal remains blocking until remediated or independently reclassified with reason/evidence. There is no deadline override. Escalate client pressure to insert unsupported facts or influence/evaluator services.

## BOQ

Confirm correct workbook/version/lot/currency and rule-pack/tender overlay. Review formula/display, hidden/merged, words/figures, totals/tax/rounding exceptions. Valo does not create or recommend rates. A waiver records owner, reason, risk and authorised approval; it does not change the original.

## Draft/red team/package

Every material factual statement must open its approved evidence provenance; unresolved placeholders block. Review diffs/comments and internal contradictions. Run the complete red-team rubric. Before sign-off, verify transactional readiness, manifest/input hashes, rendered pages, pagination/tables/cross-references, named client/Valo approvals and delivery profile. Sign only the immutable version inspected.

## Fast keyboard workflow

Use visible queue shortcuts only after verifying focus; bulk actions are for compatible non-fatal items and are server-revalidated. On partial failure, inspect each failed item. Never work around a stale-version conflict; reload/compare/reapply intentionally.
