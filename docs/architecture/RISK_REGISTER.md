## Risk handling rules

- **Open** means the material exposure remains; a planned or source-present control is not completion.
- **Mitigating** means a current control reduces exposure while named work or evidence remains.
- **Monitoring** means the control is current but needs observation/review for the listed trigger.
- **Accepted** requires a named authority, reason, expiry/review date, and residual-impact record in the catalogue.
- **Closed** requires evidence that the trigger no longer applies or the mitigation is verified; do not delete the record to make a dashboard green.

Critical risks block the affected production/GA claim unless a separately authorised, time-bounded acceptance exists. Any newly discovered tenant disclosure, stale-authority release, deletion under hold, unsupported released claim, or irreconcilable restore/deployment identity is an incident/release blocker even before the catalogue is updated.

## Review cadence

The architecture owner reviews this register at each release and no later than the earliest `reviewBy` date. Risk owners review immediately after a trigger, incident, new deployable/provider, trust-boundary change, failed quality scenario, or evidence expiry. Assigning backup owners is the first systemic action because every current row inherits `AR-001` exposure.
