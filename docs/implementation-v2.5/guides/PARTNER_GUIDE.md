# Consultancy partner guide

Partner capability is feature-flagged and is not active merely because this guide exists.

## Responsibility setup

Before access, record the partner-client relationship, client ownership, instruction/controller roles, allowed services/data, administrators, QA/co-sign responsibility, branding, revenue-report inputs, effective dates and exit/return/deletion terms. Access requires both active relationship and engagement assignment.

## Manage people and clients

Partner admins grant least-privilege partner roles within delegated scope; they cannot create Valo platform roles or access another partner/client. Revoking relationship or assignment denies access. Client owner retains visibility required by the agreement.

## Perform work

Use the same cited requirement, evidence, BOQ, drafting, red-team and fatal-gate controls. Partner analysts cannot make unverified claims claimable, bypass conflicts/entitlements, downgrade fatal findings alone or mark a Valo-branded package ready. Co-sign shows exact responsibility.

## Branding

Allowed branding changes logo, colour/type tokens and contact/presentation fields. It cannot hide Valo/partner responsibility, alter evidence/provenance/status/risk/limitations, change signed content or obscure privacy/integrity notices. Brand profiles are versioned and accessible/contrast checked.

## Reporting and exit

Usage/performance/revenue-share reports cite source period/rules and do not authorise payment. Benchmark data requires separate governed consent/privacy release. On relationship end, revoke access, reconcile active engagements/exports, return/delete data per roles/holds, preserve required audit and issue records.
